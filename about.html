About Kangroos Estate

A property partner for the decisions around the address.

Kangroos Estate Pvt Ltd is a Kharagpur-based real-estate company serving property needs across plots, construction, documentation support, infrastructure and home design.

Who we are

Local context. Clear services. Practical next steps.

Our approach is built around making property conversations easier to understand: what you are trying to achieve, what information is available, what support is relevant and what should be verified before a decision.

Our flagship project is Kangroos Estate near Changuail, Digra, Kharagpur. Alongside the project, the company provides five service areas that support different stages of the property journey.

What we focus on

A deliberately integrated service model.

Plots

Buying and selling support with clearer enquiry intent.

Construction

Planning, estimation, design coordination, execution support and finishing.

Documentation

MKDA documentation assistance, process support, coordination and guidance.

Infrastructure

Underground drainage and electric line support as supplied.

Design

Elevations, estimates, planning and design coordination.

Trust through transparency

We'd rather state what is known — and what needs verification.

Project claims, distances, facilities and other details are based on supplied marketing material and should be verified before a purchase or construction decision. No reviews, ratings, approvals, awards or financial returns are represented here without supporting evidence.
